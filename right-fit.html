Schedule a Right Fit Call

Find Out What Working Together Looks Like.

A Right Fit Call is a short, no-pressure conversation to figure out whether Winnacle Wealth is the right place for the work you're trying to do. We won't recommend anything on this call. We'll listen, answer your questions, ask a few of our own, and decide together what's next.

A Short, Exploratory Conversation.

  • You describe what you're working through.
  • We ask questions. Mostly to understand the situation.
  • We tell you, plainly, whether this is something we can help with.
  • If yes, we describe what working together would look like.
  • If no, we point you to a better-fit resource.

A Pitch. A Sales Call. A Commitment.

  • We won't recommend an investment.
  • We won't ask you to bring documents or numbers.
  • We won't ask for credit card or bank info.
  • You're not signing up for anything by booking.
  • No follow-up pressure if you decide not to engage.

Frequently Asked.

Who Do You Typically Work With?

Three groups, mostly. Pre-retirees and Central Texas families building toward (or recently in) retirement, with a 20+ year horizon. Equity-compensated professionals. SpaceX and other equity holders facing vesting, exercise, or liquidity decisions where the tax math actually matters. Women in financial transition. Divorce, widowhood, career change, taking ownership. JustHer® is the practice for these moments.

When Are You Probably Not the Right Fit?

If you're looking for stock-picking or active trading, not what we do. If you want a one-time consult with no follow-through, we do ongoing planning. If you need a planner local to you outside Central Texas, happy to refer. If you're below our typical engagement size, ask anyway; sometimes there's a better starting point (like a class).

Do I Have to Bring Documents?

No. This is a conversation, not a planning meeting. Bring whatever's on your mind.

How Much Do You Charge?

The Right Fit Call is free. Engagement fees vary by scope; if we move forward, we walk through the fee structure on a separate call before anything is signed.

Are You Fiduciaries?

Yes. Winnacle Wealth™ is a fiduciary. Every recommendation comes with the reasoning behind it.

Do I Have to Be Local to Waco?

No. Most calls happen on Zoom. We work with clients across the states where we're registered.

Can I Skip the Call and Just Send a Message?

Yes, send a note from any page footer or the contact form on the home page. We'll reply within one business day.

What Happens to My Contact Info?

It stays with us. We don't share, sell, or send marketing partner emails. See the Privacy Policy.
