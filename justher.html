Why I Built JustHer®.

I started in tax. Years in private practice, then financial planning. What I kept seeing, over and over, was women landing in front of an advisor at the hardest moment of their life and being handed a generic playbook. The advice fit a hypothetical household, not the one in the room.

JustHer® is the practice for the moments most planning misses: the divorce attorney just sent the QDRO, the death certificate just cleared probate, the equity package just vested into a life that no longer looks like the one it was sized for. It's planning that starts with listening, not with a product.

— Jessica Burch, MBA, CFP®, MBA · Founder, JustHer®
