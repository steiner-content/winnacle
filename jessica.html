About Jessica

Jessica Burch is a CERTIFIED FINANCIAL PLANNER™ professional and the heart behind Winnacle Wealth, a wealth management firm serving individuals and families across Texas and beyond. Born and raised in Texas, Jessica brings both deep roots and genuine care to every client relationship she builds.

Outside the office, Jessica is a proud wife and mom. She and her high school sweetheart, Bryan, have been married 27 years and together raised three sons. Her commitment to community runs just as deep, with over a decade of service through Young Men’s Service League, the Waco Foundation, Meals on Wheels, Shepherd’s Heart Mobile Food Pantry, and Harris Creek Baptist Church.

Jessica believes financial planning should reflect what matters most in your life. Her goal is simple: help you rise to your pinnacle with clarity, purpose, and peace of mind.
